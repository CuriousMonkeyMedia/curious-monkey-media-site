SEO in Montreal: common questions

How long does SEO take to work?

Most local businesses start seeing movement for less competitive searches within one to three months. Competitive searches such as “marketing agency Montreal” usually take six months or more.

Do I need a physical office to rank in Montreal?

No. Service-area businesses can use a Google Business Profile with the address hidden and list the areas they serve instead.

Do you do SEO in French?

Yes. We research and write French keywords and pages, not machine translations of the English site.
