Video production in Montreal: common questions

Do you film at my business?

Yes. We bring the equipment to your location anywhere in Montreal, downtown, the West Island, the East End, or the north or south of the island.

How long does filming take?

Most small-business shoots take a few hours. We plan the shots in advance so your day isn't disrupted.

Can one shoot give me several videos?

Yes. We usually cut one filming session into a main video plus several reels and short clips for social media.

Are captions and French versions included?

Yes. We add captions and can deliver English, French or bilingual versions.

How do we start?

With a free 30-minute call. We talk about your goals and you leave with one clear recommendation.
