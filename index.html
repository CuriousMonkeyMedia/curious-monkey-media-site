What we do

Social media management, content creation, website design, SEO and digital advertising for small businesses across Montreal.

Curious Monkey Media started with a simple belief: a good business should not go unnoticed just because its marketing does not show what it is worth.

Curious by name and by nature, we look closely at how each business works, what makes it valuable and where customers are slipping away. It comes from a background in business at the John Molson School of Business, plus hands-on work in content creation, website design, market research and brand strategy.

The goal: help people notice your business, understand it and choose it.
