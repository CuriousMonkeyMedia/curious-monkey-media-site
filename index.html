About

Stop monkeying around with your marketing.

Content, websites, SEO and advertising designed to help small businesses attract leads and turn them into customers.

The company

Curious Monkey Media is a Montreal marketing company helping small businesses build a stronger presence, reach the right audience and turn interest into meaningful opportunities.

We begin by understanding the business, including its goals, customers, challenges and current position. This allows us to identify what is holding growth back, whether it is limited visibility, unclear positioning, an outdated website or inconsistent marketing.

From there, we establish clear priorities and create a practical plan using the services that will have the greatest impact. This may include content creation, website design, SEO, advertising, brand development or market research.

The goal is not to add more marketing for the sake of it. It is to create a connected foundation where every decision has a purpose, every channel supports the next and the quality of the marketing reflects the quality of the business.

The founder

I'm Daniel Yoskovitch, a Montreal marketer and founder of Curious Monkey Media. I started the company because great businesses should not go unnoticed, and the right marketing helps people recognize the value they offer.

My experience spans content creation, website design, market research, brand development and campaign strategy. Curious by name and by nature, I look closely at how each business works, what makes it valuable and where potential customers may be getting lost. The goal is to connect the pieces, uncover overlooked opportunities and build marketing that gets people to notice, understand and choose the business.
